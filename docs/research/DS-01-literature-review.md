# DS-01 focused literature review
Original sources (not a systematic survey); reported results on their own tasks are not Chomp-Chomp evidence.
- Lei, Berg, Bansal, NeurIPS 2021, [QVHighlights/Moment-DETR](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/62e0973455fd26eb03e91d5741a4a3bb-Abstract.html): supervised free-form text + video features -> moment coordinates and saliency. Dataset includes human queries and saliency. Adapt interval representation; defer direct use because query-conditioned training is mismatched. [Code](https://github.com/jayleicn/moment_detr).
- Moon et al., CVPR 2023, [QD-DETR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Moon_Query-Dependent_Video_Representation_for_Moment_Retrieval_and_Highlight_Detection_CVPR_2023_paper.html): query-conditioned feature interaction, reported improvements on QVHighlights, TVSum, Charades-STA; needs video/query features and learned weights. Defer as open-world discovery backend, consider targeted investigation.
- Xiong et al., CVPR 2019, [Less Is More](https://vision.cs.utexas.edu/projects/highlights/): uses short-vs-long video duration as weak supervision; reports unsupervised highlight benchmark improvements. Experiment only with authorized domain data; duration bias is severe for edited vs raw VOD.
- Song et al., CVPR 2015, [TVSum](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2015/html/Song_TVSum_Summarizing_Web_2015_CVPR_paper.html): unsupervised title-image-based visual importance. Adapt diversity/coverage, not title relevance as entertainment.
- Zhang et al., ECCV 2022, [ActionFormer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07925): learned temporal localization from multiscale features, outputs class and interval. Defer direct training; known actions differ from unexpected highlights.
- [Lightor](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/towards-extracting-highlights-from-recorded-live-videos-an-implicit-crowdsourcing-approach/) (2020): timestamped audience chat proposes positions, implicit crowdsourcing refines boundaries; evaluated Dota2 and another game in original work. Adapt optional reaction lane; absence of chat cannot exclude moments. Delay, spam and personality sensitivity require local tests. HighlightMiner exact intended publication could not be established; no citation invented. Example [open-source clipper](https://github.com/Cbhhhh211/livestream-highlight-clipper) documents weighted chat, emotion keyword and speech fusion; no verified comparative performance.
- [YAMNet](https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet), Google/TensorFlow: MobileNetV1, 521 AudioSet labels, mono 16kHz, 0.96s patches with 50% overlap, pretrained. First DS-02 candidate; Keras compatibility caveat. General acoustic classes do not imply humor/surprise, cannot reliably separate streamer from game. CPU target to measure, no inference performed. Repository Apache-2.0 code; checkpoint distribution terms verify before shipping.
- Kong et al., 2020 [PANNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.10211), [weights](https://zenodo.org/records/3576403): AudioSet pretrained CNN variants, sound tags/embeddings, transfer to downstream tasks. Compare smaller and CNN14 variants; PyTorch, model-specific sample rates. 
- Chen et al., 2023 [BEATs](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/beats-audio-pre-training-with-acoustic-tokenizers/), [code](https://github.com/microsoft/unilm/tree/master/beats): learned acoustic tokenizers, pretrained transformer and fine-tuned classifiers. MIT code; verify weight terms. Defer heavy inference until local cost measured.
- Gong, Chung, Glass, Interspeech 2021 [AST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01778), [code](https://github.com/YuanGongND/ast): spectrogram transformer, classification, 0.485 AudioSet mAP reported for ensemble (single 0.459 in repo). Compare only with pinned checkpoints, equal hardware and intervals; no transfer claim.
- [VideoAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10517) and distinct [memory-augmented VideoAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11481), 2024: iterative visual retrieval and structured temporal/object memory for video QA. Adapt selective investigation and event memory; QA benchmark accuracy is not discovery recall. [DrVideo 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Ma_DrVideo_Document_Retrieval_Based_Long_Video_Understanding_CVPR_2025_paper.html): document retrieval for long video; defer.
- Cormack, Clarke, Buettcher SIGIR 2009 [RRF](https://doi.org/10.1145/1571941.1572114): combines ranks rather than incomparable scores; implement. Carbonell/Goldstein SIGIR 1998 [MMR](https://doi.org/10.1145/290941.291025): marginal relevance versus redundancy; adapt later with verified event similarity. Early fusion needs aligned representations and training; late rank fusion is simpler for missing modalities. Calibrated probabilities need labeled held-out data; no learned mixture-of-experts in prototype.
- Budget-aware design: coarse-to-fine retrieval, independent lane coverage and exploration as explicit cost tradeoffs, not free recall. Model invocation cost must include initial extraction and later investigation. 
No checkpoints downloaded or measured. Exact model sizes, hardware speed, model licenses (as distinct from code), recent commercial internals, and exhaustive benchmark tables are not established; record these as DS-02 follow-up, not invented values.
