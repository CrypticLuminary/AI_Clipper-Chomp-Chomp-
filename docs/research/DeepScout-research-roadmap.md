# DeepScout roadmap
DS-01 follow-up: independent authorized VOD labels; real equal-budget/equal-footage tests, provenance-aware matching, rank tie and lane imbalance stress; no production change pending review.
DS-02 SoundSense: YAMNet vs PANNs vs BEATs/AST with pinned licenses/checkpoints, measured realtime factor and held-out livestream event labels; feed acoustic Evidence. 
DS-03 Hawkeye: inexpensive visual novelty + selective frame/segment retrieval; visual Evidence, evidence cost ledger. Depends on Keystone interfaces.
DS-04 EventMemory: time-indexed utterance/visual/audio leads with verifiable links and retrieval budgets; avoid gold-derived links.
DS-05 EditorialJudge: separate pairwise preference and abstention on independently rated clips, evidence-grounded selection.
DS-06 StoryCut: use core/context distinction to retrieve setup/payoff, test boundary judgments with gold essential spans.
DS-07 BudgetBrain: optimize extraction + investigation cost, adaptive escalation and exploration only if held-out benefit justifies opportunity cost. No all-task implementation in DS-01.
